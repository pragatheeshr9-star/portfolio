## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-craft digital editorial design, balancing the tactile restraint of bespoke art monographs with dynamic digital polish. Inspired by the meticulous curation of Justine Soulié and Matt Burd, the personality is quiet, authoritative, warm, and hyper-literate. 

The aesthetic is a hybrid of Modern Luxury Editorial and Warm Tactile Minimalism:
- **Tonal Subtlety:** Vast fields of unhurried warm alabaster and whipped champagne, punctuated by hairline structures and selective luminous glints.
- **Editorial Decisiveness:** Stark, high-legibility typography anchored by near-black espresso and mineral charcoal, rejecting generic system greys for tonal warmth.
- **Atmospheric Nuance:** Rare, intentional accents of soft luminous violet and warm golden-amber that behave like light reflecting off precious metals and silk rather than digital UI indicators.
- **Craftsmanship:** Precision alignment, generous letter spacing in micro-labels, razor-sharp composition, and micro-interactions that feel deliberate and physical.

## Colors
The palette evokes archival rag paper, hand-cast bronze, and twilight sky. High-contrast legibility is preserved across all contexts while bathing the canvas in bespoke warmth.

- **Primary (`#1C1817` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded and absolute. Serves as the principal typography and structural ink color, eliminating the harsh sterility of `#000000` while delivering AAA contrast ratios over parchment surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F5EE` - Warm Alabaster):** The base background surface. Supported by `#F2ECE1` (Champagne Oyster) for secondary card structures and `#E5DED1` for delicate hairline dividers.
- **Secondary (`#8A79B9` - Luminous Violet):** Used for focal creative highlights, delicate interactive focus rings, tag pills, and ethereal hover washes.
- **Tertiary (`#C99A45` - Warm Golden-Amber):** The gilded signature. Applied sparingly to editorial milestones, awards, active metadata markers, and select featured badges.
- **Muted Oyster Grey (`#6B655F`):** Used strictly for secondary editorial notes, timestamps, and column titles to preserve clear hierarchical depth.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the tension between historical book design and modernist digital precision.

- **Editorial Headings:** `EB Garamond` brings classical restraint, humanist italics, and bespoke editorial gravity. Display titles should use sentence casing with deliberate breaks, avoiding aggressive letter wrapping.
- **Body & Functional Text:** `Hanken Grotesk` introduces crisp geometric legibility, balancing the warmth of the serif with contemporary clarity. Generous line heights ensure frictionless readability for long-form case studies.
- **Micro-labels & Index Numerals:** `Hanken Grotesk` uppercase at `label-md` and `label-sm` with deliberate tracking (+0.08em to +0.12em) provides the feel of a fine-art gallery catalog.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model prioritizes compositional tension, asymmetric balancing, and uncompromised whitespace:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`>1024px`) with `4rem` outer page margins and `2rem` gutters. Tablet devices (`768px-1023px`) utilize an 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins. Mobile devices (`<768px`) collapse into a 4-column system with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Case studies alternate between full-bleed imagery, offset 7-column content panes, and quiet 3-column metadata sidebars.
- **Breathing Rhythm:** Sections are partitioned by generous vertical spans (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) rather than heavy physical dividing walls, establishing visual pace through calibrated emptiness.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through luminous surface gradation and tonal paper layering rather than synthetic drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation rises by shifting base surfaces from Warm Alabaster (`#F8F5EE`) to Oyster Champagne (`#F2ECE1`) and lifted floating card tones (`#FCFAF6`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Modals, project panels, and header bars employ a 1px solid border rendered in `#E8E1D5` or a 10% opacity espresso overlay (`rgba(28, 24, 23, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Luminous Shadows:** Floating elements (such as lightboxes or sticky action bars) cast soft, warm-tinted shadows: `0px 16px 36px -8px rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Translucent Overlays:** Scrims and elevated navigation utilise a 85% opacity champagne surface matched with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur to preserve ambient gallery tone.

## Shapes
The shape language is quiet, architectural, and tailored:

- **Soft Contours (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive components (buttons, text inputs, chips) carry a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, conveying hand-finished cardstock rather than industrial plastic or sterile tech aesthetics.
- **Card and Image Containers (`rounded-lg`):** Feature cards and media viewports use `0.5rem` (8px) radius, preventing visual tension at media intersections while maintaining crisp editorial silhouettes.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** Flyout drawers and narrative modal sheets use `0.75rem` (12px) to communicate soft tactile layers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#1C1817`) fill with Cream (`#F8F5EE`) `label-md` text. Height 44px, padding 0 24px, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a seamless transition to a dark luminous violet-tinted charcoal (`#2A2433`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1px hairline border (`#DCD3C4`), Primary text. Hover state initiates a soft background fill of `#F2ECE1`.
- **Tertiary Link:** Inline text link styled with an animated bottom hairline in `#C99A45` (Warm Amber) that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- Height 26px, padding 0 10px, 4px corner radius.
- Background in translucent champagne (`rgba(242, 236, 225, 0.7)`), 1px border in `#E5DED1`. Text in `label-sm` tracking uppercase using Primary Charcoal or soft violet (`#6E5C9E`) for categories.

### Project & Case Study Cards
- Crisp containers with 8px radius, seamless image masking, and an unbordered image canvas.
- Typography sits decoupled below media: year/category in Oyster Grey `label-md`, followed by title in `headline-md` Garamond. Hover smoothly lifts images using a subtle 1.015x scale with 500ms cubic-bezier easing.

### Input Fields
- Understated rectangular inputs with 4px border radius.
- Default: 1px border in `#DDD5C7`, warm alabaster fill.
- Focus: 1px border in `#8A79B9` (Luminous Violet) accompanied by a 2px outer glow (`rgba(138, 121, 185, 0.2)`).

### Lists & Index Rows
- Editorial index rows separated by hairline rules (`#EFE9DE`).
- Content structured in an 8-column row: index numeral (01, 02), client name in bold serif, discipline chips, and right-aligned completion date. Row hover shifts background to an imperceptible tint of warm violet-alabaster (`#F5F1F8`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- 18px dimensions with 3px corner radius (checkboxes) or full circle (radios).
- Inactive: 1px border `#CEC4B3`. Active: Solid `#1C1817` fill housing a fine champagne check icon or centered dot. Focus states glow with subtle violet tinting.
